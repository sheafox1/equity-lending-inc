How It Works

A simple process from application to closing

We are a small business with only a few employees, so you always speak directly with us.

1. Send your deal

Fill out the loan application and tell us about the property.

2. We evaluate it

On most deals we do our own evaluation and skip the appraisal delay.

3. Inspection and title

We inspect the property and close through a title company.

4. Draws as you build

Construction draws are released on a draw schedule and inspections.
